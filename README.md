# The Long Way Back

A walking game. You wake up a long way from your couch, among wary survivors at a camp called Hollow Ash. Real walks to real parks bring back what the camp needs.

Play it on your phone: https://bronco4eva.github.io/Long-way-back/

Open the link, allow location when asked, stand at your front door and set home. The whole game is `index.html` plus the images in `art/`. Progress is saved on the phone only.
